# Contributing to HuKi

Thanks for your interest in HuKi!

HuKi is a personal project that I build in my free time, so I keep its direction and code in my own hands. It's
also my professional reference: the code here shows my own work, so it should stay written by me. That's why
**I don't accept pull requests**, and unsolicited ones will be closed without review. Please don't take it
personally: it's not about the quality of your work.

## What is welcome

- **Bug reports**: open an issue with the steps to reproduce, the platform and the app version.
- **Data fixes**: a wrong destination, description or trail detail? Open an issue and I'll take a look.
- **Ideas**: feel free to share them in an issue. I read everything, but I can't promise to build them.

## Forks

You're free to fork HuKi under the terms of its licenses. Forks must use a different name and app icon, see
[NOTICE](NOTICE), section 3.
