Discover Hungary with HuKi - an interactive map showing the Hungarian trail markings. Optimized for hiking and outdoor activities.

HuKi can help you see the hiking trails nearby, plan your next trip or set off on a GPX track downloaded from a hike collection.

HuKi features:
• Interactive hiking map with the Hungarian trail markings
• Location tracking, GPS-based navigation
• Map layers: Nature, Urban and Satellite
• Search for Places and Destinations
• GPX route import, storage and loading
• Hungary's landscapes on the map (e.g. Bükk, Mátra, Balaton), with 400+ destinations
• Destination categories, e.g. scenic railways and educational trails with built-in GPX routes
• Blue Trails: National Blue Trail (OKT), Rockenbauer Pál South Transdanubian Blue Trail (RPDDK) and Great Plain Blue Trail (AKT) on the map, divided into sections
• Blue Trail stamping points with distances and estimated travel time
• Route Planner for planning on both hiking trails and bike routes

HuKi is a free, ad-free and open-source app, built and maintained by a single developer as a free-time hobby project.
